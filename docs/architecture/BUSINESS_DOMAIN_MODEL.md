# Purpose

The Business Domain Model establishes the common business language for AIQuantTradingResearch.

It defines the core concepts, relationships, and terminology used throughout the platform.

Every architectural decision, software component, API, database schema, machine learning model, and engineering artifact should align with this shared vocabulary.

This document serves as the foundation for the project's business rules and domain-driven design.

---

# Vision

AIQuantTradingResearch is an AI-assisted quantitative research platform designed to acquire market data, transform it into meaningful information, generate trading insights, evaluate investment strategies, and support evidence-based decision making.

The platform is intended for research and experimentation. It does not provide financial advice or automated investment recommendations.

---

# Domain Boundaries

The platform encompasses the following business domains:

* Financial Markets
* Market Data
* Quantitative Research
* Feature Engineering
* Machine Learning
* Strategy Development
* Backtesting
* Portfolio Analytics
* Risk Analysis
* Performance Evaluation

The platform deliberately excludes:

* Order execution
* Brokerage integration
* Live trading
* Financial advisory services
* Regulatory compliance workflows

# Core Business Concepts

The following concepts define the ubiquitous language of the project.

## Market

A financial environment where instruments are traded.

Examples include cryptocurrency exchanges, equity markets, futures markets, and foreign exchange markets.

---

## Exchange

An organization or platform that provides market data and facilitates trading.

Examples include Binance, Coinbase, CME, and NASDAQ.

Within AIQuantTradingResearch, exchanges are primarily data providers.

---

## Instrument

A tradable financial entity.

Examples:

* Bitcoin
* Ethereum
* Apple stock
* E-mini S&P 500 Futures

Every instrument belongs to a market and is identified by one or more symbols.

---

## Symbol

The unique identifier used by an exchange to represent an instrument.

Examples:

* BTCUSDT
* AAPL
* ESZ6

Symbols are exchange-specific representations of instruments.

---

## Quote

A point-in-time market observation.

A quote may contain:

* Bid price
* Ask price
* Last traded price
* Volume
* Timestamp

Quotes represent the atomic unit of market information.

---

## Candle

A time-aggregated representation of market activity.

A candle consists of:

* Open
* High
* Low
* Close
* Volume
* Time interval

Candles are derived from quotes or trades.

---

## Market Dataset

A curated collection of validated market observations prepared for research and experimentation.

Datasets should be reproducible, versioned, and traceable to their original data sources.

---

## Feature

A measurable characteristic derived from market data.

Examples include:

* Moving Average
* RSI
* MACD
* Volatility
* Order imbalance

Features serve as inputs to machine learning models and quantitative analysis.

---

## Feature Set

A coherent collection of features used within a specific experiment or model.

Feature sets should be versioned and reproducible.

---

## Label

The expected outcome associated with historical observations.

Examples:

* Future price movement
* Return over a time horizon
* Buy, Hold, or Sell classification

Labels enable supervised machine learning.

---

## Experiment

A controlled evaluation of a hypothesis.

An experiment combines:

* Dataset
* Feature Set
* Model
* Configuration
* Evaluation Metrics

Experiments should be reproducible.

---

## Model

A mathematical or machine learning representation trained to identify patterns or generate predictions from market data.

Models are evaluated but never assumed to be universally optimal.

---

## Prediction

The output generated by a trained model for a given observation.

Predictions may represent:

* Price movement
* Probability
* Classification
* Regression values

Predictions are informational outputs, not trading decisions.

---

## Signal

A recommendation generated from one or more predictions and business rules.

Signals typically include:

* Buy
* Sell
* Hold

Signals express analytical intent rather than execution instructions.

---

## Strategy

A formal set of rules defining how signals are interpreted and evaluated.

Strategies may combine:

* Technical indicators
* Machine learning predictions
* Risk controls
* Position sizing

Strategies are evaluated through backtesting before any practical consideration.

---

## Backtest

A simulation that evaluates how a strategy would have performed using historical market data.

Backtests measure effectiveness under reproducible conditions.

---

## Portfolio

A collection of positions managed under a common investment strategy.

Portfolios provide the context for evaluating allocation and diversification.

---

## Risk

The potential for adverse outcomes associated with a strategy or portfolio.

Examples include:

* Drawdown
* Volatility
* Exposure
* Concentration
* Value at Risk (VaR)

---

## Performance

A quantitative assessment of a strategy or portfolio.

Common metrics include:

* Total Return
* Sharpe Ratio
* Sortino Ratio
* Maximum Drawdown
* Win Rate

Performance should always be evaluated alongside risk.

---

# Business Principles

The platform is governed by the following business principles:

* Data quality precedes model quality.
* Reproducibility is mandatory.
* Every prediction must be traceable.
* Models support human decision-making.
* Strategies are evaluated through evidence.
* Performance must always be interpreted with risk.
* Transparency is preferred over unnecessary complexity.

---

# Business Rules

The following rules apply throughout the platform:

* Every market observation must include a timestamp.
* Every dataset must identify its source.
* Every feature must document its calculation method.
* Every experiment must be reproducible.
* Every model must record its training configuration.
* Every prediction must reference the model that generated it.
* Every backtest must specify the dataset and strategy used.
* Every reported metric must identify its calculation period.

---

# Future Evolution

This document will evolve as new business capabilities are introduced.

Changes to the Business Domain Model should be carefully reviewed because they may affect architecture, APIs, persistence models, machine learning workflows, and engineering documentation.

Maintaining a consistent ubiquitous language is essential to the long-term sustainability of AIQuantTradingResearch.

# Guiding Statement

Software should faithfully represent the business it serves.

A clear, shared business language enables better architecture, clearer communication, more maintainable code, and more reliable engineering decisions.

The Business Domain Model is the bedrock upon which AIQuantTradingResearch will be designed, implemented, and evolved.
