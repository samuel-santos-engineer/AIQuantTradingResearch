
# Architectural Principles

**Status:** Draft
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-09
**Owners:** AIQuantTradingResearch Team

---

# Purpose

Architectural Principles define the enduring engineering values that guide the design and evolution of AIQuantTradingResearch.

They provide a consistent foundation for evaluating architectural decisions, resolving trade-offs, and maintaining coherence across the platform as it grows.

Unlike implementation practices, these principles remain stable over time and apply regardless of technologies, frameworks, or programming languages.

---

# Vision

Every architectural decision should strengthen the platform's ability to evolve, remain understandable, and produce trusted engineering knowledge.

Architecture is measured not only by technical quality, but also by how effectively it enables long-term collaboration, reproducibility, and sustainable innovation.

---

# Principle 1 — Business Before Technology

Technology exists to support business capabilities.

Architectural decisions should begin with business goals rather than implementation preferences.

Business concepts should remain stable even as technologies evolve.

---

# Principle 2 — Architecture Before Implementation

Design should precede implementation.

Architectural understanding reduces complexity, improves communication, and minimizes costly redesign.

Implementation should realize architectural intent rather than define it.

---

# Principle 3 — Simplicity Before Cleverness

Prefer the simplest solution that satisfies the problem.

Complexity should only be introduced when it delivers clear and measurable value.

Readability is more valuable than novelty.

---

# Principle 4 — Modular by Default

Business capabilities should be implemented as cohesive, loosely coupled modules.

Modules should evolve independently while communicating through explicit contracts.

Modularity improves maintainability, scalability, and collaboration.

---

# Principle 5 — Explicit Boundaries

Architectural boundaries should be clearly defined and respected.

Responsibilities, ownership, and dependencies should remain explicit rather than implicit.

Well-defined boundaries reduce accidental coupling.

---

# Principle 6 — Trust Through Governance

Trust is established through disciplined engineering practices.

Governance applies to architecture, code, data, documentation, and research assets.

Every important engineering artifact should be traceable and governed.

---

# Principle 7 — Reproducibility First

Research results should be reproducible.

Experiments, datasets, configurations, and engineering decisions should be versioned and documented.

Reproducibility is essential for scientific credibility and continuous improvement.

---

# Principle 8 — Knowledge is a Strategic Asset

Knowledge generated by the platform should be preserved, organized, and discoverable.

Architecture, documentation, experiments, and datasets represent long-term organizational assets rather than temporary deliverables.

Engineering should continuously transform information into reusable knowledge.

---

# Principle 9 — Technology Independence

Architectural decisions should remain independent of implementation technologies.

Frameworks, libraries, cloud providers, and programming languages are replaceable.

Business capabilities and architectural intent should endure.

---

# Principle 10 — Observability by Design

Operational visibility should be designed into the platform from the beginning.

Systems should communicate their health, behavior, performance, and quality through meaningful telemetry.

Observability improves confidence, diagnosis, and continuous improvement.

---

# Principle 11 — Evolution Over Perfection

The architecture should support continuous refinement.

Incremental improvements are preferred over infrequent, disruptive redesigns.

Architectural evolution should be intentional, documented, and aligned with long-term goals.

---

# Principle 12 — Engineering Excellence

Quality is a shared responsibility.

Architecture, implementation, testing, documentation, and operational practices should collectively reflect professional engineering standards.

Engineering excellence is achieved through discipline, collaboration, and continuous learning.

---

# Applying the Principles

Architectural principles should guide decisions at every level of the solution, including:

* Business capabilities
* Module design
* Public contracts
* Technology selection
* Dependency management
* Data governance
* Research workflows
* Engineering processes

When principles conflict, contributors should evaluate trade-offs transparently and document significant decisions.

---

# Architectural Decision Filters

Before introducing a significant architectural change, contributors should consider:

* Does this strengthen business capabilities?
* Does it simplify the solution?
* Does it improve modularity?
* Does it reduce coupling?
* Does it preserve explicit boundaries?
* Does it improve reproducibility?
* Does it increase confidence?
* Does it create reusable knowledge?
* Can it evolve without widespread redesign?
* Is the added complexity justified?

These questions encourage consistent and thoughtful architectural evolution.

# Future Evolution

Architectural principles are expected to remain stable.

Changes should occur only when the project's long-term direction fundamentally evolves.

Any modification should be carefully reviewed, documented, and communicated, recognizing that these principles influence every architectural decision within the platform.

---

# Guiding Statement

Architecture is the disciplined practice of making decisions that remain valuable long after technologies change.

The Architectural Principles of AIQuantTradingResearch ensure that every contribution strengthens a platform built for clarity, adaptability, reproducibility, and enduring engineering knowledge.
